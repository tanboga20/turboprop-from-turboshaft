# Phase notes: Phase 0 and Phase 1

## Phase 0 (literature, requirements; SRR)
- Customer: Heron TP-class MALE UAV (MTOW 5,670 kg, >30 h, >45,000 ft, 220 KTAS, single pusher at rear); today PT6A-67A 1,200 shp, 229.5 kg dry.
- Core: T700-class gas generator + free 2-stage PT, 22,000 rpm (CT7-9 value); T700-GE-700 1,622 shp (135% of 1,200 shp).
- Ratio 12.94:1 (22,000 -> 1,700 rpm) below reference range 15.4-21 (PT6A-67A 17.6, CT7-9 15.9, PW127 15.4-17.2, TPE331 20.9).
- 7 architectures from patents/NASA: coaxial 2-stage planetary (PT6A), offset parallel (PW100), offset first stage + planetary (US 7,144,349), tower-shaft offset (US 6,735,954), split torque (US 9,611,789), four layshafts (US 6,264,138), differential planetary (NASA CR-179625).
- T700 facts: front drive, PT shaft through GG, both rotors supercritical (CR-180899), A/B/C sumps, emergency air-oil mist in A and B sumps, 3 um filter, integral oil tank 6.9 L, modular.
- Certification basis CS-E + 14 CFR 33 (A-01): CS-E 510/33.75 (hazardous effects <=1e-7..1e-9, single failure 1e-8), 515/33.70 critical parts, 840/850/33.27 overspeed after shaft break (CM-PIFS-003), 525/33.74 continued rotation, 570/33.71 oil, 820/33.84 over-torque (15 min hot oil), 650/33.83 vibration, 740/33.87/33.93 150 h endurance + teardown, 180/33.95 propeller functioning, 80/33.25 accessories, 33.19(b) -> Part 35.
- Requirements v1: 53 req in 11 groups (SYS, LD, SAF, DYN, STR, LUB, ACC, PIF, CTL, MNT, ENV), 8 needs, 10 assumptions. Key numbers: 5,027 N m prop torque (MCT), 388 N m PT, transient 6,915 N m (137.5%, 20 s, PT6A-67A), 20 kN thrust both ways, gyroscopic 7.2 kN m (Ip 15 kg m2, 2.5 + 1.0 rad/s, 14 CFR 23.371), +3.17/-1.27 g, SYS-05 98.5% (13.6 kW), SYS-06 100 kg (CT7-9 365.1 - T700 198 = 167 kg scaled by torque 0.56 -> 93-105 kg), TBO 4,000 h / life 20,000 h, ACC-02 generator 52 kW pad.
- SRR decisions (8): LD-01 137.5%/20 s; LD-02 over-torque at LD-01; LD-05 rates; no reverse thrust (PIF-04); LUB-07 30 min goal; ENV-01 53 C flat rating; STR-05 4,000/20,000 h; ACC-02 52 kW.
- Pusher options: A reversed core, B extension shaft, C rear drive (T700-701K precedent).

## Phase 1 (concept; PDR)
- 1.1 Installation C (rear drive, PT shaft out the back like T700-701K), score A 66 / B 56 / C 67. Precedents TPF351-20 (CBA-123 Vector, 11.4:1, 1,700 rpm), AlliedSignal US 4,892,269 & 4,930,725 (exhaust ejector around hub; hot exhaust ahead of prop -3..7% prop efficiency). 701D vs 701K: power -4..-7%, SFC +2.4..3.2%, +21 kg, +44 cm. Cycle model: 1% inlet loss -> -1.7% power, +0.7% SFC; C costs +2-3% SFC (35-55 min on 30 h mission).
- 1.2 RGB architecture O (offset parallel helical first stage + planetary second stage), score O 78 / P 75 / S 66. Key reason: P's fast first-stage planets carry centrifugal ~9.4 kN > 7.0 kN gear load -> journal bearings needing uninterrupted oil (EP 2 799 674) -> kills LUB-07/08; O gives accessory pads from first-stage gear, pitch oil at shaft inner end, opposite hand with two idlers (US 7,144,349), T56 precedent 13.54:1 (NASA TM-83716, TM-2007-215019: planet bearings ~90% of predicted failures). Price ~+7 kg gears, 150 mm offset, 87 m/s pinion PLV.
- 1.3 Preliminary sizing MATLAB tool (simplified ISO 6336:2019, ISO/TR 1281-1 checked vs BSI texts): full search 6,367 tooth sets -> 5,733 valid designs; 3 independent implementations (MATLAB, Octave, Python), 109 test values. Five planets 78.7 kg; 4-planet 0.4 kg heavier.
- PDR choice: four planets 31/110 | 45/37/119 (hunting teeth), ratio 3.55 x 3.64, post gap 38 mm (18 with five), K_gamma 1.15.
- 1.4 Separate RGB oil system (score 87 vs hybrid 60, shared 53); losses 12.76 kW (98.67%); 4 accessory pads (generator z57, OSG, PCU, oil pumps) 4.04 kg; RGB 77.6 kg. Shaft-break: detect 14.5 ms, fuel cut 39.5 ms, peak 123.3% (tau 0.1 s) -> SAF-06 on core. Propeller shaft: no forward-whirl critical; backward whirl crosses 1x just below steady range -> damper (DYN-05). Oil 15.1 L/min need 22.1, pump 28.7 L/min, scavenge 71.8, tank 6.66 L.
- Requirements v2: 65 req (12 new, 10 changed), Agreed 27, TBR 25, TBD 13. K_A 1.25. Propeller opposite to PT (baseline).
